DEUCLE

How to play

Guess the word in six tries. Every guess must be a real five-letter word; only the answers are tennis terms.

  • S S is in the word and in the right place.
  • E E is in the word but somewhere else.
  • R R is not in the word at all.

A new word every day at midnight, local time.

Also daily: Gridle (Formula 1), Brickle (IndyCar)
